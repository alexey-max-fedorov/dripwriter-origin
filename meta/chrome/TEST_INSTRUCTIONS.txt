1. Open https://docs.google.com/document/create in Chrome.
2. Click the Dripwriter Origin icon in the toolbar.
3. Paste or voice dictate text into the popup (voice dictation uses OS accessibility tools, not the extension).
4. Click inside the document, then click Start.
5. Confirm text types out character by character at a natural pace.
6. Click Stop — typing halts immediately.
7. Adjust sliders, restart — confirm new settings apply.
8. Reopen popup — confirm settings persisted.
