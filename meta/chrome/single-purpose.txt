This is an accessibility tool for people who cannot type comfortably or continuously due to motor impairments, repetitive strain injury, essential tremor, or similar physical conditions, by simulating natural human typing in Google Docs on behalf of the user.. These users compose text via voice dictation, AI writing tools, or other adaptive input methods, then use Dripwriter Origin to enter that text into Google Docs with realistic keystroke simulation — preserving their ability to participate in collaborative document workflows without performing sustained physical typing.

The extension performs one action only: accepting text from the user and typing it character by character into the active Google Docs document, with configurable speed, typo, and break patterns that simulate natural human input. It does not browse, read, track, or modify any other content on any page.
